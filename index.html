Metalwork

Magle Signs and Fabrications is a specialized architectural metalwork firm. We partner with architects and designers to provide engineering design-assist and custom metal fabrication for luxury residential and commercial architecture.

Residential

Residential metalwork shows up in the details people live with every day. It shapes how a space feels when you move through it, not just how it looks in a photo.

Magle supports residential projects where bespoke eco screens, custom gates, and structural steel elements need to align with architecture from the start. We stay involved early so design intent carries through fabrication and installation without compromise.

Explore Residential

Commercial

In commercial spaces, metalwork has to do more than complete a design. It has to hold up to use, coordination, and schedule pressure without losing precision.

Magle works directly with project teams to fabricate architectural metalwork—from heavy-duty sliding gates to illuminated signage—that fits into complex builds and performs in demanding environments.
